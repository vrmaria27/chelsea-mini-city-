Welcome to Chelsea Mini City

  1. Choose a building.
  2. Point at an empty plot.
  3. Build a happier Chelsea.

Homes start at 50 happiness; a shop within 80 m adds 10 and each park within 80 m adds 10. Hover a plot to see the exact effect before you buy. Use Inspect to see why a building is (or isn't) doing well, and to upgrade it to Level 2. Work through three neighbourhood chapters; income arrives every 10 seconds of play.
